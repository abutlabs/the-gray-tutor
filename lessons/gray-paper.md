# The Gray Paper, part by part

The Gray Paper comes in sections, §1 to §22, and appendices, A to I. The tutorial follows
the order a client needs them in, not the paper's: the codec of Appendix C before the
header of §5, the PVM of Appendix A in [Part 15](./15-the-pvm/index.md). So every lesson says at its top which
sections it covers, and every part says so under its name in the sidebar.

Here is every section, and the lessons that cite it. Tap a lesson to go to it. A section
no lesson covers is either not a rule (the introduction, the thanks) or something a
validator does beside importing blocks, which is what your client is for.
