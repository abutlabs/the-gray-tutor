# The Gray Paper, part by part

**In one sentence:** every section of the Gray Paper, and the lessons that cover it.

The Gray Paper comes in sections, §1 to §22, and appendices, A to I. The tutorial follows
the order a client needs them in, not the paper's: the codec of Appendix C before the
header of §5, the PVM of Appendix A in [Part 15](./15-the-pvm/index.md). So every lesson
says at its top which sections it covers, and every part says so under its name in the
sidebar.

Tap a lesson to go to it. A section no lesson covers is one of two things: not a rule
(the introduction, the thanks), or something a validator does beside importing blocks,
which is what your client is for. To read the paper's own lines, open
[Reading the Gray Paper](./reading-the-gray-paper.md) first.
